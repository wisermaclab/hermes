# HERMES 0.2.0 development validation report

Date: 2026-10-23. This is development evidence from a dirty checkout, not a certificate for a tagged release. The controlled release-reference check and per-job source provenance are reported separately.

## Controlled release-reference check

Two fresh native runs each used one fixed source snapshot, with 34 jobs across
the smooth and diffuse synthetic plate fixtures (PO once, RT and Hybrid PO over
eight seeds). Every PO complex-ADC comparison and RT/Hybrid PO seed-mean power
comparison passed, with zero observed difference. The frozen reference is labeled
as a controlled development reference, not as a historical release.
[Detailed regression metrics and provenance](0.2.0-regression.json) are retained.

## Analytic verification

Native CPU float64 PO, 60 GHz, ideal PEC, unit gains, 1 km monostatic range; no fitted amplitude. The 152 × 152 mm plate is evaluated at 0°, 0.5° and 1° while refining 2–512 faces. At 512 faces the analytic facet integrator has relative RCS errors of 0.000939%, 0.000631% and 0.0631%, respectively, against the far-field closed form. Residual finite-range effects are retained. Centroid integration converges more slowly near the 1° null.

The 15 mm-radius sphere uses 1,020–16,380 faces and approaches the optical-limit RCS with 0.0581% relative difference at the finest mesh. This checks the high-frequency PO model, not exact Mie scattering.

## RT ray-density diagnostics

Plate and square-trihedral cases use 60 GHz, 50 mm sides, 5 m range, five seeds and 1,000–1,000,000 rays/source, with up to three specular interactions. The plate is missed at 1,000 and 10,000 rays; at 100,000 and 1,000,000 rays its equivalent RT RCS is about 78.54 m² versus 3.146 m² from the PEC plate formula. The corner returns no paths in this test. These are disclosed model/sampling limitations, not successful RCS validations. An all-zero seed interval does not establish zero physical scattering.

## Measured copper plate

All 16 source files from the 2026-08-12 capture group match the SHA-256 values in the prepared bundles. The model uses the supplied 152 × 152 mm plate geometry, approximately 0.668 m broadside range, IWR6843AOP waveform and finite copper conductivity. TX order, phase/IQ conventions and parts of the placement geometry remain provisional. These measurements are raw ADC; an older note calling them averaged is not used as a processing instruction.

The statistic is mean linear per-channel peak range-profile power in 0.5–0.8 m, with a Blackman window and 1024-point FFT. A single offset at 0° is retained for all other angles. No absolute gain accuracy is claimed.

[Detailed per-fixture/solver metrics and provenance](0.2.0-copper-plate.json) include all 176 native seed jobs. The development sweep spans several source snapshots as provenance/reporting code was edited; per-job hashes are retained and this mixed sweep cannot be frozen as a release reference. A tagged release must regenerate evidence from one fixed source snapshot.

The smooth synthetic plate is specular and has no observed RT seed spread. The diffuse synthetic fixture does: mean RT FFT power is about 2.126×10⁻⁶ with a 95% seed-mean Student-t interval [2.062×10⁻⁶, 2.190×10⁻⁶] across eight seeds. These units are native FFT power, not calibrated watts. Seed intervals exclude measurement and model uncertainty.
